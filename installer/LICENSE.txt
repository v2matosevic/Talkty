PRIVACY

Local transcription processes audio on your device. Cloud transcription and
Prompting are optional: enabling them sends the relevant audio or text to
OpenRouter and its model providers and costs per use. Optional prompt planning
and fidelity checks can send transcript/prompt text to a decision model.

With transcription during pauses enabled, a selected cloud model can receive
audio before you press Stop. One additional early request may be billed even
if you cancel or continue speaking. Local models still keep audio on this PC.

Settings, transcript history and diagnostic logs are stored on this PC. Logs
can contain dictated text. The OpenRouter key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
At Stop, cloud recordings are saved encrypted for retry and removed after
successful history saving or when you choose Discard. Early in-flight audio
is not a persistent recovery copy. Model downloads and update checks use the
network. Talkty does not send analytics or telemetry.

Optional command mode sends the transcript and captured window information to
a separately configured service on this PC. That service controls any further
actions or network access. It is not bundled with Talkty.
